Returns & Exchanges

A considered experience, from order to aftercare.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

7-day return window: Please contact us within 7 calendar days of delivery to request a return or exchange.

Eligibility

Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, undamaged, in their original condition, with original tags, packaging and hygiene seals intact. Proof of purchase or your order number is required. For hygiene and intimate-wear safety, bras, panties, lingerie sets and other intimate apparel can only be returned if completely unused and the original hygiene seal and tags are intact.

Non-returnable items

Items that have been worn, washed, altered, damaged after delivery, missing tags or packaging, or have a broken/removed hygiene seal cannot be accepted for a change-of-mind return. Any statutory rights for defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods remain unaffected.

Damaged, defective or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate resolution in accordance with applicable law.

How to request a return

  1. Email lunerastore26@gmail.com within 7 calendar days after delivery.
  2. Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return and the reason.
  3. Wait for our return instructions before sending anything back. Unapproved or collect-on-delivery parcels may be refused.
  4. Pack the eligible item securely with its original tags and packaging.

Refunds and exchanges

After the returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you whether the request is approved. Approved refunds will be made using an appropriate method coordinated with you; for Cash on Delivery orders, we may request bank or UPI details solely to process the refund. Please allow a reasonable processing period after approval. Exchanges are subject to stock availability.

Shipping charges

Return shipping arrangements and any applicable costs will be confirmed when your request is reviewed. If an item is defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied, we will work with you on an appropriate remedy in line with applicable law.

Contact

For return or exchange support, email lunerastore26@gmail.com and mention your order number.

This policy does not limit any consumer rights available under applicable Indian law.
