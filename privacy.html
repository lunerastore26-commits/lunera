Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how LUNÉRA handles information when you browse our website, create an account or place an order. Please read it before using the store.

Information we collect

When you register or order, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account identifier, order items, selected sizes, order value and order status. Phone sign-in may involve a verification code. We do not ask you to provide card or bank passwords.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To process, deliver and support orders.
  • To show your order history and provide customer support.
  • To send order-related notifications and service updates.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access.

Service providers

Our store uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore to support accounts and order records, Cloudinary to host product images, and EmailJS for order notification emails. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

We use access controls intended to limit customer order access to the relevant signed-in customer and authorised store administrators. No website or online storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please keep your sign-in credentials and verification codes private.

Cookies and browser storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember your shopping bag and selected store preferences. Clearing browser storage may remove those locally saved details.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the store and its customers. We retain account and order information for as long as needed for store operations, customer support and applicable legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to information we must retain for legal, security or order-record purposes. You can also stop using your account and store services.

Children

This store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email lunerastore26@gmail.com.

This policy is a store information notice and should be reviewed for compliance with the laws applicable to your business before launch.
